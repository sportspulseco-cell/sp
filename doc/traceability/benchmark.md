# SportsEngine replacement benchmark for PPHL release 1

Written 2026-10-24 on branch `pphl-journey-fixes`. Research only: no app code, tests or data were changed.

**What this is.** A sourced comparison of what an adult ice-hockey league operator gets from SportsEngine HQ / Registration / Tourney and from four open-source league systems, against what SportsPulse has today. Use it to find gaps before PPHL leaves SportsEngine.

**Inputs.** `doc/traceability/build.py` (modules M1 to M22, FR tiers), `doc/traceability/MATRIX.md` (generated 2026-10-24; statuses copied verbatim, treated as data), the PPHL requirements document sections 3.1 to 3.21 and the PPHL use-case document (UC-01 to UC-33), plus the repo itself for section C.

**Evidence levels.** *fetched* = the page was opened on 2026-10-24 and states the behaviour. *unverified* = seen only in a search snippet, an article title in a fetched index, or a PDF that would not decode. Nothing below is inferred from marketing copy alone; where a product page is the only source it is named as such. SportsPulse statuses come from MATRIX.md; SportsPulse rule verdicts in section C come from `rg` over `apps/` and `packages/` with file:line cited.

**Shorthand for SportsEngine sources.** `H<id>` = `https://help.sportsengine.com/en/articles/<id>` ; `T<id>` = `https://tourney-help.sportsengine.com/en/articles/<id>`. Intercom resolves the id without the slug. Full URLs with titles are in section D.

**A caution on the incumbent.** SportsEngine's HQ page now announces PlayMetrics, and a help article modified 2026-10-21 (H17603167) tells HQ customers how to export members, teams/games/events and financial reports "for a seamless transition" to PlayMetrics. No sunset date is stated. PPHL should be asked what they have been told; it affects how fast release 1 must land and what export formats we must import (gap B-15).

**Note on MATRIX.md statuses.** Several "Broken" rows are route 404s from the staging crawl taken *before* the CONSOLE-1 to CONSOLE-7 fixes (commits 3ad966b, c66e81f, not yet deployed when the crawl ran). They are copied as-is; re-run the crawl after deploy.

### C.3 What the engines must store because of C.2

- **Points**: per division or season, values for regulation win, OT/SO win, OT/SO loss, regulation loss and tie, with presets 2-1-0 and 3-2-1-0. A tie is a real outcome. Today `ppw/ppl/ppt/ppotl` are request parameters and OT losses are never detected (C.1 R1, R2).
- **Game result**: an `ended_in` (regulation / overtime / shootout) flag and per-period scores (periods-won tie-breaker), goal clock times (quickest first goal), penalty minutes and game-misconduct counts per team.
- **Tie-break**: an ordered, pluggable list with per-step scope (head-to-head games only vs all games), the 3-way-tie restart rule, capped GD and capped GF stored per game separately from raw totals, quotient with divide-by-zero ranking highest, and an audited manual coin flip. Division config already holds the list; the engine must read it (C.1 R5).
- **Forfeits**: configurable recorded score (0-5, 1-0, 5-0), counts as a loss, optional "cannot advance" flag, games-played credit to the non-forfeiting roster, optional fee invoice.
- **Roster**: freeze date per season with an admin override reason, a substitute relationship that survives the freeze, dressed-skater caps per game, a goalie-only slot, and "subs cannot replace suspended players".
- **Games-played credit**: position-specific (skater vs goalie), tied to a finalized sheet, compared against season thresholds, visible to captains as "playoff-qualified".
- **Suspension ledger**: triggers from penalty codes (any game misconduct, second major, match), progressive counters per season across teams, PIM thresholds per team, "served with the team where incurred" plus a league-wide ineligibility flag, carry-over into playoffs and the next season, a remaining-games countdown that follows the team's schedule, and a match-penalty state that blocks play until a reinstatement date is entered.
- **Levels**: highest rated level per player and the permitted downward range (skater 1, goalie 3 to 5), sub check-in blocked outside the range, per-division per-player goal caps enforced at scoring time.
- **Membership**: a 14-character confirmation number per season (9 digits + 5 letters, reject the XX variant), name and DOB match, two-season window April to August, expiry 31 August, and a separate per-league-season "waiver on file" flag.
- **Scheduler**: slot length with warm-up inside it, different OT/shootout formats for regular season, elimination and championship games, curfew logic.
- **Refunds**: injury credit by games remaining, a non-refundable flag for disciplinary removal, refund capped at amount paid; pro-rate-with-admin-fee is a configuration option, not the US adult default.
- **Age gates**: 18+ at registration for adult leagues (21+ for Classics); no minor pathway in adult leagues without an affiliate-specific rule, although the funnel's guardian-consent step is correct for youth programs.
